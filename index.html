    Inherent tier comes from criticality, data sensitivity and access. Residual tier lowers it by up to two levels based on the questionnaire score; a “No” on a key control keeps the vendor at Medium or above. Unanswered questions count as not demonstrated. Reviews are scheduled by inherent tier: Critical every 6 months, High 12, Medium 24, Low 36.

    Company logo

    Your organization's logo, shown in the top bar, on the dashboard, on the executive briefing and posture snapshot PDFs, and on vendor questionnaires. PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG; a wide logo on a transparent or white background works best.

    Only admins and owners can change the logo.

    Internal standards

    Load one or more files of your internal standards: JSON, CSV (with a Standard column) or plain text with one per line. The standards from all loaded files form the Internal standard dropdown on risks, and the AI reviews use them. Loading more files keeps the list already loaded.

      Only admins and owners can change the standards.

      Threat posture assessment framework

      The NIST framework your threat posture assessments follow. It sets how security controls are grouped and which reference each one shows: CSF 2.0 categories and subcategories, or SP 800-53 control families and controls. Answers are kept if you change it, and completed assessments keep the framework they were completed under.

      Only admins and owners can change the framework.

      Vendor sections and fields

      Sections and fields add extra questions to the vendor editor. Other is included to start with. Add sections and fields as you need them.

        Only admins and owners can change the sections.

        Risk Ledger sections

        Sections group the fields in the risk editor. Other is included to start with. Add sections and fields as you need them.

          Only admins and owners can change the sections.

          Vendor questionnaire

          The security questionnaire vendors complete from their link. Reword the built-in questions, turn off the ones you don't need, and add your own questions and sections. Changes apply to every vendor the next time their questionnaire is opened.

          Only admins and owners can change the questionnaire.
